THE FIELD GUIDE

See a place.
Find its words.

01 · Enter the arenaThe host creates a room. Players enter the same five-character code on their own devices. You can also practise solo.

02 · Race the clockEach of the eight photos has 20 target descriptors. You have 90 seconds. Type one word or compound adjective at a time, then press Enter.

03 · Choose preciselyTargets are ranked from 100 to 5 points by vocabulary challenge. Each player can score every target once. Another player’s answer never takes your points.

04 · Follow the cluesMeaning clues appear at 30 seconds; first letters at 55; second letters at 75. Your unsolved words get the hints. Points stay the same.

05 · Read the moodAfter each round, review the scoreboard, all 20 answers, their meanings and possible connotations. The host starts the next photo. Highest total after round eight wins; equal scores share a place.

These are curated targets, not the only valid descriptions. Connotations change with context. The points reward vocabulary challenge, not a word’s universal “quality”. British and American spellings are accepted where listed.
